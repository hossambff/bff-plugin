# BFF house style

Seeded from the Notion "Brand Writing Guidelines" (Marketing, last edited May 2023). **Status: draft — needs a refresh by the team.** Person-specific voices (partners' LinkedIn posts, BFF articles) live in their own skills and take precedence for those formats.

## Voice
BFF sounds like a passionate, experienced early-stage investor in blockchain and emerging tech: knowledgeable, forward-looking, practical, supportive of founders.

- **Clear before clever.** Plain words, short sentences, active voice, verbs over nouns. Explain jargon or drop it.
- **Reader first.** Lead with what matters to the reader (LP, founder, partner), then the detail.
- **Positive, not hype.** Celebrate real wins with specifics (numbers, names, milestones). No empty superlatives.
- **Transparent.** Say who owns what and what happens next. No vague hedging.
- **Inclusive.** Write for a global, multilingual audience; avoid idioms that don't travel.

## Format
- Subheadings and short bullets for anything longer than a few paragraphs.
- Numbers: specific and sourced (e.g. "$4.2M ARR at Dec 2025, up 3x year on year"), currency stated.
- Emojis: sparingly, never in formal, legal, LP or sensitive messages.

## By audience
- **LPs / LPAC:** measured, factual, numbers first, risks stated plainly. Use "(Scrubbed)" versions for wider distribution; never include unscrubbed portco data outside the intended audience.
- **Founders / portcos:** warm, direct, actionable next steps.
- **Public / social:** enthusiastic but concrete; check portco names and claims are public before posting.

## Never
- Share confidential portco metrics, LP identities or deal terms in external drafts unless the operator confirms they're cleared.
- Give investment advice in public-facing content.
